Open source · Playwright

Scraper template that survives real sites

Why: most scraper scripts die on the first timeout and refetch everything after a crash. I kept rebuilding the same safeguards, so I published them instead of hiding them.

What's inside: retries with backoff, resume from CSV/JSONL, robots.txt enforced by default, deduplication, Excel-safe CSV and JSONL export, Docker, 21 unit tests, CI on every push.

Proof: github.com/svyatoslavr2000-pixel/playwright-scraper-template — one command against the demo site returns 30 rows.

30 rows · 3 pages · 0 errors in 5 s. The second run with --resume adds zero duplicates.
